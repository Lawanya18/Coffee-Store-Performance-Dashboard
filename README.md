## ☕ Coffee Store Performance Dashboard

Interactive Excel Dashboard for Sales, Profitability & Business Performance Analysis

---------------------------------

## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end data analysis workflow using Microsoft Excel.

The objective was to analyze coffee store business performance by exploring sales trends, product profitability, regional performance, budget achievement, and inventory levels. The final outcome is an interactive dashboard that supports business decision-making through KPIs, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers.

## 🎯 Business Problem

The management of the coffee store needs a single dashboard to monitor overall business performance.

The dashboard should answer questions such as:

- Are sales increasing over time?
- Which products generate the highest profit?
- Which states perform the best?
- Are actual sales meeting budget targets?
- Which markets contribute the highest profitability?
- How can business performance be improved?

## ✅ Project Objectives


- Analyze monthly sales performance.
- Compare budget sales with actual sales.
- Identify high-performing states and markets.
- Evaluate product profitability.
- Monitor inventory performance.
- Build an interactive dashboard using Excel.
- Generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

## 📂 Dataset Information

## 💡 Key Insights

- California is the highest revenue-generating state, making it the company's strongest-performing market.
- Green Tea is the only product generating a loss, suggesting pricing, production costs, or promotional discounts should be reviewed.
- Sales peaked in July, indicating seasonal demand or successful business performance during that month.
- Actual sales consistently outperformed budget targets throughout the year, indicating strong overall business performance.
- Columbian is the most profitable product and also delivers one of the highest margins, making it a key contributor to business profitability.
- Espresso is the highest-selling product category, reflecting strong customer demand.
- Major Markets contribute the majority of total sales, highlighting their importance to the company's revenue.
- The Central Market generates the highest profit, making it the most profitable region despite differences in sales performance across markets.

## 🛩️ Business Recommendations

- Continue investing in marketing and inventory in California to maintain market leadership.
- Review Green Tea's pricing strategy, production costs, and promotional discounts to improve profitability before considering product discontinuation.
- Analyze the factors contributing to July's peak sales and replicate successful marketing strategies during other high-potential months.
- Reassess future sales targets to make them more challenging and realistic.
- Increase inventory availability and promotional efforts for Columbian products to maximize revenue and profitability.
